One app the whole home shares

Today, at a glance

Open Arrow and the day is already there: who fed Luna and when, whose turn the walk is, the next pill and her weight. No group chat, no “did you…?”.

Everyone in the home sees the same screen, live.

No more double dinners

When someone already fed Luna, Arrow stops you before you log it again and says who did it and how long ago. One tap to cancel, one to log anyway.

Try it on the phone. Works for walks, teeth and grooming too.

Turns, and asking nicely

Walks and meals rotate between people, so it's never always the same one. Stuck at work? Ask “can you take tonight?” and it's handed over when they accept.

The reminder moves with the duty.

Reminders for the right person

Only whoever is on duty gets the ping. The moment anyone marks it done, it disappears from every phone, and the rest get a quiet “Olia gave the pill at 20:03”.

Medication reminders are free forever.

Health in one place

Weight over time, every dose given and by whom, and the vet's papers in your pocket. When weight creeps up, Arrow gently suggests other ways to show love than treats.

Every tip links to its veterinary source. Never a diagnosis.

Train together, step by step

24 reward-based lessons play like stories: one moment per step, what your hand does and when to say “yes”, with a timer so sessions stay short.

Whoever practises logs it, so the next person picks up there.

See who does what, fairly

A friendly weekly card shows each person's share of walks, meals and training. Not a scoreboard, just enough to notice when one person is carrying the dog.

Questions families ask

CameraIs Arrow a pet camera like Barkio?

No. A pet camera is for when you're away. Arrow is for every day when you're all home: who fed, walked and gave meds, and whose turn is next. There is a home camera inside Arrow too, but it is one feature, not the product.

FamilyWho can join my home?

Anyone you send the link to: a partner, kids, grandparents, a dog walker or a sitter. Sitters get a guest link that ends on the date you choose.

AndroidDoes it work if my partner has Android?

Yes. Arrow is an iPhone app, and anyone on Android or a computer joins the same home in the browser and sees and logs the same day.

HealthDoes Arrow give medical advice?

No. Arrow reminds and records; it never diagnoses. Every health tip links to its veterinary source, and anything worrying is a question for your vet.

PriceWhat stays free?

One home, two pets, two people, daily logging, the first eight lessons and medication reminders. Medication reminders stay free forever.
