Shared pet care

Who fed the dog? Now everyone knows.

Arrow is a shared pet-care hub for the whole household. Everyone sees who already fed, walked and medicated your dog — and what is still left to do today.

Get Arrow Try the free tools first

“Did you feed him?” “I thought you did.”

Most dogs in a shared home are looked after by three or four people, and none of them has the full picture. So the dog gets two breakfasts, or none. The tablet gets skipped because everybody assumed somebody else had handled it. One person ends up doing every single walk and slowly resents it. And the sitter has no idea what the routine is.

A pet camera tells you what is happening while you are out. Arrow is for the other twenty hours — when you are all home and still not sure whether the dog has eaten.

What Arrow actually does

One Today screen

Fed, walked, medicated, done by whom and when. The whole household reads from the same page instead of from four different memories.

Reminders that know whose turn it is

The nudge goes only to the person on duty. When they mark it done it clears for everyone — and if nobody does, it escalates to the next person rather than silently expiring.

Duties you can hand over

“Can you take the evening walk?” → accept, decline, or offer another time. The duty and its reminder move with the answer. Swap days outright when it suits.

Double-feed protection

“Luna was fed 40 minutes ago by Max. Log anyway?” One sentence that prevents a surprising amount of accidental overfeeding.

Guest access for a sitter

A link that works for one to fourteen days, carrying your routine and the buttons to tick things off. No account, no group chat, no handwritten note on the counter.

Works on Android too

If one parent is on Android they join through the web app from day one. A hub that only half the household can open is not a hub.

And yes — a home cam too. It is one feature among many, not the point of the product. If what you need is a camera and nothing else, there are cheaper ways to get one.

Three free tools, no sign-up

Potty break calculator
