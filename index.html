A day in Luna's life

Everything about your dog, in one place the whole family shares. Watch one ordinary Tuesday.

07:10 Wake up

Good morning, Luna.

Her photo breathes softly at the top of Today, over four little meters: food, walk, water and play. One tap answers “How is she today?”, and a line under her name tells you how her day is going.

A skipped day never breaks the streak.

07:45 Breakfast

Max fed her. Everyone knows.

Food, water, walks, meds and weight: Luna's whole day on one screen. The moment someone does something, the rest of the family sees it, wherever they are.

No group chat. No “did you…?”

07:57 Second breakfast?

Not today, Luna.

Olia goes to fill the bowl. Arrow stops her gently: Max fed Luna 12 minutes ago. One tap to cancel, one to log anyway.

Works for walks, teeth and grooming too.

08:25 Leash time

Bags, water, leash, treats.

A gentle nudge arrives before the usual walk, with a four-item checklist at the door. Nobody turns back halfway down the street for the bags.

Remind me 10, 15 or 30 minutes before.

08:58 The Turia gardens

Every walk, live on the map.

Arrow draws her route as you go, with the time, the distance and your pace. Tap Pee or Poo where it happens, count the treats, slip in a minute of loose-lead training, and ask for the nearest drinking fountain.

Real public fountains, from open city data.

09:10 Home again

A walk worth sharing.

Each walk becomes a card for the family chat. The week adds up in kilometres, with her personal bests and favourite spots, and the stretch by your front door is always hidden.

Only your home ever sees your routes.

12:30 Stuck at work

Pass the leash, not the guilt.

Walks and meals take turns, so it's never always the same person. Max asks “Can you take the evening walk?”, Sofia taps Accept, and the reminder moves with it.

Sharing the care is as easy as sharing the love.

13:10 Lunch

Down to 12 kg, together.

One weight goal for the whole home, at a safe 1–2% of her weight a week. Everyone feeds the same way: the portion on the kitchen scales, treats under a tenth of the day’s calories.

Agree the goal with your vet first.

16:00 After school

Sit. Step two. Good girl.

24 photoreal lessons play like stories: one filmed step at a time and a timer, so ten minutes stay ten minutes. Whoever practises logs it, and the next person picks up from there.

Training becomes a family thing.

18:40 Uh-oh

She found the chocolate.

Panic gets an answer in one tap: how much is dangerous for her weight, the poison helpline for your country, and the emergency vets open right now, with directions.

Nobody can pay to be listed here.

19:15 The vet's papers

Finally, in plain English.

Arrow explains any vet document simply: what the vet found, what to do, when to go back. Every line quotes the paper, and medicines are copied word for word into a reminder you confirm.

Only once you switch it on. Never a diagnosis.

20:04 Heart pill

One pill. One person reminded.

Tonight it's Olia's turn, so only Olia's phone buzzes. At 20:03 it's done, and everyone else gets a quiet “nothing for you to do”.

Medication reminders are free forever.

21:05 Bangs outside

Fireworks. She stays calm.

Calm guides for fireworks, storms, alone time, the car, the vet, visitors, the vacuum and city fiestas. Before a loud date, Arrow gives you a heads-up days ahead.

Comforting her never makes fear worse.

21:30 Before bed

A healthy dog is a whole day, not one number.

Every walk and game the family logs adds up to Luna's week of activity, right next to her weight, each dose and who gave it, her vaccines and the vet's papers. A quieter week shows up early, so you can ask your vet in time.

Every tip links to its veterinary source.

11:20 Saturday in town

Other homes love her too.

Walk photos stay in Luna's gallery, just for your home, location stripped. Share one and other families see only the photo, her name and the city. This week: a Halloween costume contest, one vote each, medals for the top three. Her medals unlock a gift from a local pet shop too.

Never a route. Never an exact place.

Questions families ask

CameraIs there a camera in Arrow?

Yes. Turn a spare phone into a home camera and see your dog live, with sound and alerts when something moves. It's one part of Arrow: the rest is for every day you're all at home, from who fed, walked and gave meds to whose turn is next.

FamilyWho can join my home?

Anyone you send the link to: a partner, kids, grandparents, a dog walker or a sitter. Sitters get a guest link that ends on the date you choose.

AndroidDoes it work if my partner has Android?

Yes. Arrow is an iPhone app, and anyone on Android or a computer joins the same home in the browser and sees and logs the same day.

WalksDoes Arrow track walks?

Yes. Start a walk and Arrow draws the route live with time, distance and pace. Mark pee and poo where they happen, count treats, find the nearest public drinking fountain, and see the week in kilometres with personal bests. The first and last stretch by your home is never shown, and only your home sees your routes.

CalmCan Arrow help with fireworks and storms?

Yes. Calm guides cover fireworks, storms, alone time, the car, the vet, visitors, the vacuum and city fiestas: what to prepare, what to do on the day and what never to do. Arrow gives you a heads-up a few days before loud dates like New Year's Eve. If fear is severe, the guide tells you when to see your vet.

WeightCan the whole family help with her weight?

Yes. Set one weight goal for the home, and Arrow keeps the pace safe at 1–2% of body weight a week, with tips everyone follows: weigh the portion on kitchen scales and keep treats under 10% of daily calories. Agree the goal with your vet first.

HealthDoes Arrow give medical advice?

No. Arrow reminds and records; it never diagnoses. Every health tip links to its veterinary source, and anything worrying is a question for your vet.

PriceWhat stays free?

One home, two pets, two people, daily logging, the first eight lessons and medication reminders. Medication reminders stay free forever.

CommunityWho sees the photos I share?

Only your home, until you choose otherwise. Walk photos go into your pet's gallery, and the location is stripped on your phone before anything is saved. Share one with Community and other families see just that photo, your pet's name, a caption and the city: never a route, an exact place or anyone's name. You can take a post down whenever you like, and a post reported by three homes is hidden until it's checked.
