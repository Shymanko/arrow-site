Three free dog care tools

No sign-up, no email wall, nothing uploaded. All three run entirely in your browser and work whether or not you ever install Arrow.

Potty break calculator

Get an age-appropriate interval and a printable daily schedule in about ten seconds. Free, no sign-up.

Dog feeding calculator

Daily calories from the standard veterinary RER/MER equations, converted into grams of your actual food.

Chore chart maker

Build a printable week chart with everyone's name on it. Rotates jobs fairly, prints on one page.

Why we gave these away

Every one of them answers a question we watched households ask each other in group chats: how long since she went out, how much is he actually supposed to get, and whose turn is it. You can answer all three with a calculator and a piece of paper. Arrow exists for what comes after — keeping the whole household in sync on the answer, every day, without anyone having to be the one who remembers.

What happens to your data

Nothing is sent to us. The calculators compute in the page. The chore chart saves your household names in your own browser’s local storage so the form is pre-filled next time, and clearing your browser data removes it. There is no account and no tracking of what you typed.
