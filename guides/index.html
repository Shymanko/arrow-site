Not veterinary advice. Everything on this site is general information for healthy adult pets, compiled from the veterinary sources cited on each page. It cannot account for your dog’s breed, medical history or medications. Always check with your own veterinarian before changing food, water, exercise or medication — and call them immediately if something seems wrong.

How this content is made. Pages are drafted with AI assistance and fact-checked against the named veterinary sources before publication. We do not publish reviews, testimonials or user stories. See our editorial standards.
