# Pricing — Arrow: Family Pet Care

Status: pre-launch. Arrow is not yet on the App Store. The prices below are the
planned launch prices for in-app purchase. There is a waitlist, not a pre-order,
so nothing on this page can be bought today.

Platform: iPhone (iOS). Anyone on Android or a computer joins the same home in a
browser and sees and logs the same day.

Currency: USD. Markets: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia,
Ireland, New Zealand.

Last updated: 2026-10-27

## Free

- Price: $0
- Limits: 1 home, 2 pets, 2 people
- Includes: daily logging of feeding, walks and medication; today's shared view;
  the first 8 of 24 training lessons; medication reminders

## Plus Family

- Price: $39.99 per year, or $4.99 per month
- Trial: 7 days free
- Covers: the whole household on one subscription, through Apple Family Sharing
- Limits: unlimited family members, guests and sitters; unlimited documents
- Includes: everything in Free, plus turns, hand-offs and swap requests; care
  statistics over weeks and months; all 24 training lessons; the home camera

## Free on every plan, forever

- Medication tracking and its reminders. This is not a trial and does not expire.

## What Arrow does not do

- Arrow does not give veterinary or medical advice and does not diagnose. The
  in-app AI assistant only explains a document a vet has already written, with
  citations back to that document, and helps prepare questions for a visit.
- Arrow is not a pet camera product. A home camera is one feature of Plus
  Family, not what Arrow is for.
